# About me
I am a political scientist completing my PhD at LMU Munich. My research focuses on small states and fully self-governing territories. I also love teaching.

Over the past five years, I've designed and taught three undergraduate courses at LMU as sole instructor, each refined across several semesters. All are taught in English to mixed groups of German and international students. Together, they span comparative politics, international relations, and political economy.

My work engages with the cases that standard cross-national datasets tend to leave out, which means they're also largely left out of what we know about democratisation and democratic breakdown. My dissertation systematises this gap and documents the resulting selection bias in the research. It also argues that small democracies rarely break down because their compact size produces tightly embedded, highly visible elite and social networks that enforce norms informally, even where formal institutions are weak.

Testing arguments like this required data that did not exist, so I've built it. The first step was the Self-Governance and Sovereignty Dataset (SGS),
which extends standard sovereignty criteria to include fully self-governing cases. Because it identifies polities by self-governance rather than just sovereignty, it's relevant to almost any cross-national research where formal sovereignty isn't important, not just democracy. The full dataset will be released alongside the accompanying paper, but you can explore it now in the [Sovereignty Timeline](/sovereignty-timeline/).

The [Small State Coverage Project](/data/sscp/) extends widely used datasets to these polities. I've also built a GDP coverage tool (in preparation for release): code that combines existing GDP sources to give the widest possible coverage, and can be set up to match the sources used in a study being replicated. Including these cases changes some conclusions the literature has treated as settled and raises new questions.

I work mainly with large-N quantitative methods, though my master's research on small states in WTO dispute settlement was based on expert interviews.

Before my PhD, I completed my BA, Honours, and MA in Political Science and International Relations at the University of Canterbury in New Zealand, along with a Graduate Diploma in Economics from Massey University.
