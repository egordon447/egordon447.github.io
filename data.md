# Data

Alongside my research, I build and maintain data resources for the study of small states and fully self-governing non-state territories which are systematically excluded from most sources.

## Small State Coverage Project (SSCP)

An ongoing project extending existing political science and economics datasets to small polities that are systematically excluded from them.

[Read more →](/data/sscp/)

## Self-Governance and Sovereignty Dataset (SGS)

The Self-Governance and Sovereignty (SGS) dataset records when 224 polities became fully self-governing and sovereign, from 1800 to 2026. You can explore it in the [Sovereignty Timeline](/sovereignty-timeline/). The full dataset will be released when the accompanying paper is published.
